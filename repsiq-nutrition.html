WHAT IT DOES / 01

Measured, not guessed.

Photo calorie apps removed the search step but kept the real problem: the number depends on who's guessing. Repsiq Nutrition measures volume — so the same meal gives the same answer, and the kitchen scale can stay in the drawer.

01

Portions, measured

Point your iPhone at the plate. On models with a LiDAR sensor, Repsiq Nutrition measures the portion's volume instead of estimating it from a flat photo. You confirm what it is; it handles how much.

02

Same plate, same number

Scan the same meal twice and you get the same result, because volume is measured rather than inferred. For a casserole, a stew or a plate in a restaurant, that's the difference between a log you trust and one you abandon.

03

Leftovers subtracted

Didn't finish? Scan the plate again and what's left comes off your log. No mental arithmetic, no deleting and re-entering the meal.

04

No red days

No red-green scale, no confetti, no streak to lose. A quiet daily view that treats a different day as just a different day.

05

Menus and meal plans

Scan a restaurant menu before you order, plan the week ahead, and export the plan to your grocery basket so the food you planned is the food you bought.

06

Built for DE, AT and CH

German-language from day one and built around how people in Germany, Austria and Switzerland actually eat and shop, not translated from a US product database.

HOW IT WORKS / 02

Photo. Measure. Confirm.

Three steps. No scale, no scrolling through a database for the closest match.

  1. 01

    Photograph the plate

    One shot from your phone, the way you'd photograph it anyway.

  2. 02

    The portion is measured

    The depth sensor turns the photo into a volume, and the volume into a portion size.

  3. 03

    Confirm the dish

    Tell it what the meal is — it already knows how much. Didn't finish? Scan again and the rest is subtracted.
