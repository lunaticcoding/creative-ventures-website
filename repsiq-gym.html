WHAT IT DOES / 01

You don't have to tell it what's there.

Every training app can filter by equipment — after you've ticked sixty boxes describing your gym. Repsiq Gym removes that step, then keeps the plan honest about what's actually on the floor, who's standing on it, and what hurts.

01

It knows your gym

Pick your studio and Repsiq Gym works out which machines are actually there. No equipment checklist to fill in before your first session.

02

Plans built from what's there

Your plan only ever uses equipment your gym has. Travelling, or switching studios? It rebuilds around the new floor instead of prescribing a machine that isn't in the building.

03

Machine occupied? Swap.

Someone's camped on the leg press. One tap swaps in an alternative from the same gym, not a generic substitute from a global list.

04

Train around what hurts

Tell it what's sore or injured and the plan routes around it, session by session, instead of pretending you didn't say anything.

05

No streak drama

Missed a day? The week gets rebuilt. No guilt screen and no broken chain — one skipped session shouldn't cost you the habit.

06

A coach you can talk to

Ask out loud, mid-session, why this exercise, what to swap or how to progress, and get an answer you can act on before the next set.
